KeyOdds

How hard is a randomly generated password or passphrase to guess? Nothing you enter leaves this page, and you do not type a real password here.

About this

Entropy is length × log2(pool size), and each extra bit doubles the guesses. It is only valid when the password is picked at random by a generator. A password a person made up, like a pet name with a 1 on the end, is far weaker than its length suggests. Six words from the EFF dice list are 7,7766 = 221,073,919,720,733,357,899,776 possibilities, about 277, per EFF. Guess rates are illustrative assumptions, not measurements, and the time shown is the average, half the keyspace. Use a password manager, and turn on two-factor sign-in. About
